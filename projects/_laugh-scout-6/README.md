# Laugh scout 6: October Terms 2000 to 2004

Scout 4 stopped at October Term 2005, and the queue had fallen to five weak cases. On 3 Nov 2026 `archive_scan.py` scanned the next ground back, October Terms 2000 to 2004: 380 transcripts, none missing (raw data in `scan/archive.json`, `scan/scan.jsonl`). Re-run with `python3 archive_scan.py projects/_laugh-scout-6/scan 2000 2004`; it only scans what's missing.

The top 60 by laugh count were checked against `made.json` and the earlier scouts (one repeat, Ashcroft v. Raich, dropped), then the setup lines of the 22 with an everyday object were read and each laugh was classed object/situation or process. Counts of object/situation are from the setup lines only (not the audio), so the pick should still hear the room before building.

Audio: supremecourt.gov only hosts argument audio from October Term 2010, so every case below takes its audio from the National Archives or Oyez (logged as the source).

Left out as political, sensitive or pure process: Virginia v. Black, Lawrence v. Texas, Hibbs, Earls, Booker, Sabri, Small, Rousey, Lidster.
