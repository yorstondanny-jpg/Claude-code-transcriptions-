Recast animation pilot: a 1950s social-guidance film, to be performed by snails. The narrator spends
14 minutes lecturing a teenage girl, Barbara, in the second person about her bad habits while her
neighbour Helen does everything right.

## Candidates

Three public-domain Prelinger films with deadly serious, now-absurd narration. All three items carry
the same rights statement, `licenseurl: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain/` ("Public
Domain" on archive.org), with no other rights field, checked 2026-10-05 through archive.org's metadata
API.

Why *Habit Patterns*: it's nearly all narration, and the narrator addresses Barbara directly, with the
cold disappointment of a parole officer. *Dating: Do's and Don'ts* was also transcribed for
comparison. It's the more famous film, but this item is the incomplete Kodachrome print (the whole
carnival date is missing; from 9:25 to 10:33 Whisper hears only two stray "Thank you"s), and it's mostly acted dialogue rather
than narration. Best lines there: "How do you choose a date? Whose company would you enjoy? Well, one
thing you can consider is looks." (1:12 to 1:20) and the three rehearsed ways to say good night
(10:47 to 12:02). *Table Manners* is narrated by Emily Post (per the item description). It was
downloaded but not transcribed.

Other public-domain Prelinger items found with the same rights statement: *Good Table Manners* (1951,
GoodTabl1951), *Johnny Learns His Manners* (1946, JohnnyLe1946), *Mind Your Manners* (1953,
MindYour1953), *Shy Guy* (1947, ShyGuy1947, with a young Dick York) and *Going Steady?* (1951,
GoingSte1951). *Are You Popular?* (1947) only turned up as raw-reel items without a license URL, so it
was left out.
