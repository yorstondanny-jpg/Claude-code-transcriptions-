# Laugh scout 5: the next tier of scout 4's archive

Scout 4 scanned every transcript from October Term 2005 to now and its queue ran dry (one case left, Georgia v. Randolph, which the pick had already judged a weak, door-search case). So on 18 Oct 2026 the 5 to 10 laugh tier of `_laugh-scout-4/archive.json` was read: cases not made, not on scout 4's skip list, argued October Term 2010 or later (the Court's own audio). Each laugh was classed object/situation or process by reading the setup lines, for about 40 of the 169 cases.

Most of this tier is process: justices teasing lawyers about precedents (Nelson, Matlock, Gloucester) and procedure. The ones with a viewer-sized stake are below. Tyler v. Hennepin County (11 laughs, only 2 pure object, but a $15,000 debt, a $40,000 condo and a unanimous give-back ruling announced on tape) was picked and transcribed as `tyler-surplus/`.
